Process

Three steps. About four seconds.

No pairing screens, no network setup. The whole exchange happens in the time it takes to bump two phones together.

01

Open the file

Pick anything — a photo, a PDF, an APK, a whole folder. ANRO-BEAM reads it straight from your file system.

02

Touch the phones

Hold the two devices back to back. ANRO-BEAM detects proximity and opens a direct link between them.

03

Confirm and beam

Tap once to send. The file moves phone to phone, never through a server, and lands ready to open.

Why it's different

Everything stays between the two of you.

Built for the moment you just want to hand someone a file, the way you'd hand them a photo print.

Works offline

No Wi-Fi, no mobile data, no router. ANRO-BEAM talks phone to phone directly.

Any file type

Photos, video, documents, APKs, whole folders — if it's on the phone, it can be beamed.

Point-to-point only

Files travel directly between the two devices. Nothing is copied to a server in between.

No account, no wait

There's no sign-up screen and no queue. Open the app and it's already listening.
